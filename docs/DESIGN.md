# Direction brief
Subject: April, a temporary-facility inquiry site for project and operations managers.
Direction: Industrial field journal with precise equipment planning.
Palette: ink navy #102f3a for structure, bright paper #ffffff for clarity, mist #edf3f4 for quieter sections, safety orange #be4022 for actions, slate #4c636b for secondary text, line #b9c9ce for dividers.
Type: self-hosted Barlow Condensed bold for equipment-scale headings and Barlow regular/semibold for readable operational details; system fallback. Fluid display scale, fixed body size.
Layout: compact utility bar and navigation; asymmetric headline + original vector facility illustration; dense service list; spacious planning spread; closing inquiry panel.
Signature: numbered equipment index paired with a technical facility illustration, explicitly conceptual.
Omitted: unverified fleet photography and manufactured trust claims.
Mobile: headline precedes art; service rows stack; modal menu is keyboard accessible; planning columns become a sequence. Primary inquiry stays visible. 320/390/768/1280 verification required.

This is an original April design derived from the reference industry and broad service categories. It does not reproduce the source website's layout or assets. All copy remains draft until business review. Concept illustrations are code-native SVG, not actual fleet images or engineering drawings.
