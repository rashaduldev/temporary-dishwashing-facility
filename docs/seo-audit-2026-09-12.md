# Temporary 123 SEO audit and implementation

Checked 12 September 2026, Singapore time.

## Executive diagnosis

The canonical business origin is `https://temporary123.com`. It still serves the older WordPress website. The redesigned site is available at `https://temp123-alpha.vercel.app`, which is a revision hostname. The Vercel pages intentionally remain `noindex` until the primary domain is moved and the migration release gate passes. Allowing both hosts to index the same material would split canonical signals and expose an incomplete migration.

The main availability risk is the old production site. Existing migration evidence records many `500`, `404` and timed-out legacy routes. The redesigned build now has a complete, testable URL inventory: 635 generated pages, one real 404 document and 59 direct or patterned permanent redirect rules. All 102 unique paths in the backlink export are either served directly or mapped in one redirect to a relevant live page.

A fresh raw-HTML sample of 100 production URLs recorded 30 non-`200` responses and 67 fetch or robots failures. The crawl reached its 100-URL limit, so these numbers describe the sample rather than the full WordPress inventory. The production `robots.txt` request was forcibly closed during the sample.

Google index status and Google-selected canonicals remain unknown because no Search Console property was available in this task. A successful crawl or sitemap entry does not prove indexation.

## Duplicate and content decisions

The recovered source review found two exact empty-body duplicates. `/about-us/` now has useful original copy. `/blog/` remains accessible but is excluded from the future index until real posts exist. `/video/`, `/26ft-military-bulk-kitchen/`, `/4000-correctional-facilities-series/`, `/government/hospitals/` and `/modular-kitchen-facilities/` are also withheld from the future sitemap because their recovered bodies are insufficient.

`/planning-tool/` redirects to `/planning/`, `/shop/` redirects to `/equipment-rental/`, and `/contact/` redirects to `/contact-us/`. Internal links point directly to the preferred destinations. Location pages remain a major content-review area. The current 625-page sample does not justify a quality conclusion for the reported 98,253-page WordPress inventory.

## Architecture and rendering

Every generated page is discoverable through ordinary HTML links. Core pages are linked from navigation or the footer. Equipment pages are linked from the equipment directory and to related items. Recovered routes are linked from the service and location directory. Important headings, copy, links and metadata are present in initial HTML without requiring JavaScript.

The homepage keeps its high-priority image eager and responsive. Other images are lazy loaded with dimensions. The entry script is 5.62 KB raw and 2.23 KB gzip. CSS is 34.63 KB raw and 7.48 KB gzip. These are lab implementation observations. Field LCP, INP and CLS remain unknown until enough production traffic is available.

## Validation

The local production-style build generated 635 pages plus a real 404. The SEO acceptance check inspected 636 HTML documents, 14,802 local links and 1,236 local image references. It found 636 unique titles, 636 unique descriptions, no broken local destinations, no orphan pages, no links to redirects, no heading errors and no conflicting draft canonical or sitemap signals.

The browser reachability suite verified all 635 generated routes and all 102 backlink paths. The complete browser suite passed at desktop and mobile widths. Handler/import tests, database security rules, the native Vercel runtime boundary checks and the secret pattern scan also passed.

The Vercel revision remains intentionally excluded from indexing. On primary-domain release, production mode will emit absolute self canonicals, `index,follow`, a canonical-only XML sitemap, Organization and WebSite structured data on the homepage, and breadcrumb structured data on eligible inner pages. Six explicitly insufficient pages remain `noindex,follow` and outside the sitemap.

## Remaining unknowns and monitoring

Before primary-domain cutover, recover and reconcile the remaining WordPress inventory, review location-page uniqueness, verify all high-value legacy mappings, and confirm DNS, TLS and host redirects. The release gate correctly blocks indexing while migration status is 625 of 98,253.

On release day, crawl the primary hostname and check robots, status, canonical, sitemap, rendered mobile content, structured data and every backlink redirect. After 24 to 72 hours, review Search Console sitemap processing, Page Indexing and representative URL Inspection results. Review crawl errors, canonical selection, clicks, impressions and Core Web Vitals weekly during stabilization, then reconcile inventory and duplicates monthly.
